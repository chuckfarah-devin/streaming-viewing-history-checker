# Streaming Viewing History Checker

An Android application that answers **"Have I watched this before?"**

Point your phone at a television, photograph the title on screen, and the app checks your imported Netflix viewing history to tell you whether you have previously watched it — and when.

**Phase 1:** Netflix viewing history on Android (Samsung).

**Method:** Specification-Driven Development (SDD).

See [`specs/`](specs/) for the Business and Technical Specifications.
